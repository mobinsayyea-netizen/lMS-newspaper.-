News in your language, by topic. Built for listening with TalkBack.

AI options (API key)

Paste an OpenRouter or Google Gemini API key to translate stories into your chosen news language, get short summaries, and use Explain. The key is saved on this device only, so you enter it once.

    Built for screen readers like TalkBack. Each story is tagged with its own language, so your screen reader switches pronunciation automatically. The app's menus and buttons always stay in English; only the news language changes.
